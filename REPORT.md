## 🧠 Bot ne kya seekha (aagey isi hisaab se topics/andaaz chunega)

- Space and defense/robotics topics generate the strongest engagement and views, with Europa Clipper reaching 157% retention and Combat Robots achieving a channel-high 1,941 views, whereas environmental/earth science topics (e.g. Icebergs, Colorado River) stalled below 600 views.
- 'Myth vs fact' is the most effective Shorts style for retention, averaging over 87% view percentage (peaking at 113.8% on Artemis III and 88.8% on Maglev), significantly outperforming 'Future scenario' formats (~58% retention).
- Direct channel plugs and cross-promotions cause severe retention cliffs; directing viewers to a full channel breakdown caused an immediate 23.4% drop at second 38.6 on the Jet Engines short.
- The critical drop-off window occurs between 4.4 and 8.3 seconds across almost all Shorts, with abstract or slow contextual hooks losing up to 9.2% of viewers instantly compared to punchy factual hooks.
- Closing calls to action completely fail to drive engagement; 18 out of 24 videos received zero comments, indicating end questions need stronger debate-inducing framing rather than passive closes.
- Long-form data is currently too thin to draw definitive topic conclusions (only 4 uploads with 25-36% retention), whereas Shorts consistently achieve distribution, with 70% of uploads crossing 1,000 views.

## 🔎 Viral Shorts ka mutala (2026-11-02, 12 videos)

Is mahine viral hone wali videos mein strong visual shocks, counter-intuitive hooks, aur clear step-by-step explanations ka pattern sab se zyada chal raha hai. Hamare apne analytics yeh dikhate hain ki viewers shuru ke 5 se 8 seconds mein drop ho rahe hain agar topic ya hook mein thodi bhi confusion ho. Humein apni shuruat ki lines ko aur zyada sharp aur simple rakhna hoga bina lambe complex sentences ke. Is ke ilawa, aakhri sign-off mein Tomorrowly ka naam alag aur fresh andaz mein use karna zaroori hai taake audience bore na ho. Visuals aur 3D animations ki synchronization ko mazeed behtar banakar hum apne watch percentage ko aur upar le ja sakte hain.

**Script writer ke naye usool:**

- State the topic clearly within the first 3 seconds to avoid early drop-off.
- Keep total video length strictly between 20-25 seconds for news updates and 30-35 seconds for big questions.
- Make numbers feelable by linking them immediately to a concrete, visual scale instead of just quoting raw statistics.
- Use a counter-intuitive hook or striking visual question in the opening line to drive initial engagement.
- Structure the narrative as a clear step-by-step evolution from a problem to an unexpected technical solution.
- End every Short with a fresh, natural sign-off mentioning Tomorrowly instead of repetitive catchphrases.
- Avoid complex multi-clause sentences in the first 7 seconds where drop-off rates are historically highest.
- Prompt viewer interaction with a targeted, specific question at the end rather than a generic subscribe plea.
