## 🧠 Bot ne kya seekha (aagey isi hisaab se topics/andaaz chunega)

- Space and astronomy topics drive the highest retention and scores, with Europa Clipper (154.9%), Voyager 1 (121.7%), and Artemis III (114.1%) leading the channel.
- Generic channel call-to-action outros ('Follow Tomorrowly for tomorrow's tech, today') consistently cause immediate drop-offs of 4% to 6.3% between 18s and 26s.
- The 'Shocking fact first' style generates the strongest average reach (averaging over 1,300 views on Shorts) and high engagement, including the top-performing Short (1,971 views).
- Long-form content suffers from severe discoverability issues, averaging under 185 views (with two videos under 10 views) and low retention (25-46%), indicating thumbnails and hooks need work.
- Drop-offs consistently spike between 4.5s and 8.5s across Shorts when transitioning from the hook into technical details, showing the early hook-to-body handoff needs tightening.
- Comment engagement is practically zero across almost all videos (22 out of 25 Shorts have 0 or 1 comment), showing end-screen call-to-actions and discussion prompts are failing.
- The 'What if' style shows high retention (60% to 121%) but struggles with distribution/clicks (under 300 views across both examples), suggesting conceptual titles are less clickable than concrete news hooks.
- Data is too thin to reliably evaluate 'teaser' format or deep-dive long-form formats due to small sample sizes (1-2 videos each).

## 🔎 Viral Shorts ka mutala (2026-11-09, 12 videos)

Is mahine viral hone wali shorts mein yeh baat samne aayi hai ke shuru ke 3 sehon mein hi asal topic ya hairat-angez sawal wazeh kar dena sab se zaroori hai. Hamari apni videos mein 5 se 8 seconds ke darmiyan log drop ho rahe hain, is liye hamein apni opening lines ko mazeed taiz aur dilchasp banana hoga. 3D cutaways aur real-world comparisons (jaise cheezon ko aam cheezon se compare karna) viewers ko aakhir tak bandh kar rakhte hain. Hamein apni fixed timing (news ke liye 20-25 seconds aur big questions ke liye 30-35 seconds) par sakhti se amal karna chahiye. Aakhir mein ek behtar aur naya sign-off istemal karen jo Tomorrowly ka zikr kare aur comments ko barha sake.

**Script writer ke naye usool:**

- Name the core subject or state the primary paradox within the first 3 seconds to clear up any ambiguity immediately.
- Keep script pacing between 2.5 to 3.0 words per second to match the viewer's natural digestion speed for complex science topics.
- Make abstract numbers tangible by comparing them to everyday objects like pickup trucks, football fields, or transit times.
- Maintain our fixed duration limits strictly: keep news shorts between 20 to 25 seconds, and big question shorts between 30 to 35 seconds.
- End every script with a fresh, natural sign-off that mentions Tomorrowly without repeating previous catchphrases.
- Ensure all technical and scientific claims are 100% factual while explaining them using simple, everyday language.
- Structure the mid-section as a step-by-step breakdown or progressive scaling sequence to maintain high dopamine and retention.
- Design the final sentence to either loop seamlessly back into the opening hook or leave a powerful lingering paradox.
