## 🧠 Bot ne kya seekha (aagey isi hisaab se topics/andaaz chunega)

- Shorts with 'news' and 'big question' formats consistently break 1,000+ views (averaging ~1,100 to 1,340 views), whereas niche environmental or speculative topics like cloud seeding stall under 200 views.
- Promotional callouts and long outtros trigger heavy drop-offs; pointing to full channel videos caused an immediate 23.4% retention drop at 38.6s in the Jet Engines teaser.
- Viewer drop-off spikes sharply in the first 4 to 8 seconds across almost all Shorts (losing 3% to 9% instantly), showing hooks need to deliver the payoff faster without slow preamble.
- Style 'Myth vs fact' achieved the channel's highest retention (117.74% view percentage and 1.41 score on Artemis III), outperforming 'Before vs now' and early 'Future scenario' styles.
- Engagement remains critically low with near-zero comments (0 to 1 per video), confirming that current closing questions fail to trigger viewer discussion or debate.
- Long-form content has very low traction overall (under 60 views across 2 of 3 uploads), but specific engineering questions with strong curiosity titles ('Why Don't Jet Engines Melt at 2,000°C?') can achieve breakout traffic (509 views), though the sample size (3 videos) is too small to declare a reliable trend.

## 🔎 Viral Shorts ka mutala (2026-10-26, 12 videos)

Is mahine wohi shorts zyada viral hui hain jinhon ne shuru ke 3 seconds mein koi hairan kun baat ya visual shock diya hai. Hamari purani videos mein log shuruat mein ya ajeeb aakhri sawal par drop ho rahe hain, is liye humein bekaar intro khatam karke direct topic par aana hoga. Har video ka time fix rakhna zaroori hai (news ke liye 20-25 seconds aur big questions ke liye 30-35 seconds). Aakhri line hamesha 'Follow Tomorrowly for tomorrow's tech, today.' hi honi chahiye aur complex numbers ko aam cheezon ke sath compare karke feelable banana hoga.

**Script writer ke naye usool:**

- State the core topic or paradox within the very first sentence without generic introductory fluff.
- Hook the viewer immediately in the first 3 seconds using a stark visual shock, a counterintuitive contradiction, or an urgent question.
- Ground massive or microscopic numbers immediately by comparing them side-by-side with familiar physical objects like cars or football fields.
- Never hide the core product, subject, or app name until the very end; introduce it early to maintain high retention.
- Strictly adhere to our fixed time limits: exactly 20 to 25 seconds for news items, and 30 to 35 seconds for big questions.
- Structure the middle beats around a fast escalation, step-by-step mechanical breakdown, or multi-tier progression.
- Replace abstract philosophical closing questions with concrete statements, visual climaxes, or our mandatory outro line.
- Always conclude every single video with our fixed sign-off line: 'Follow Tomorrowly for tomorrow's tech, today.'
